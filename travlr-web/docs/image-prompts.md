# Travlr image prompt set

The public landing and Explore pages use the following assets, generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and copied into `public/images/`. The shared visual direction is photorealistic editorial travel photography: natural light, candid human-scale moments, tactile detail, warm but restrained color, and no visible text, logos, or watermarks.

- `hero-travel-planning.webp` — Two friends planning a trip at a sunlit café table, an open city map and notebooks in the foreground, a Lisbon hillside and water view through a large window, candid over-the-shoulder composition, documentary travel editorial photography.
- `destinations/tokyo.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Tokyo at blue hour, a lively neighborhood street with warm storefront light, layered city texture, a few distant pedestrians, atmospheric but uncluttered, no readable signage or logos.
- `destinations/lisbon.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Lisbon, terracotta rooftops and a bright waterfront view from an elevated neighborhood, late-afternoon sun, tiled architecture, calm and inviting, no readable text or logos.
- `destinations/reykjavik.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Reykjavik, colorful low-rise waterfront buildings with open sky and distant Icelandic mountains, crisp cool daylight, quiet sense of adventure, no readable text or logos.
- `destinations/mexico-city.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Mexico City, leafy historic streets and expressive architecture in warm daylight, layered neighborhood life without crowding, rich but natural colors, no readable text or logos.
- `destinations/cape-town.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Cape Town, dramatic mountain and coastal landscape with a walkable foreground, clear golden light, expansive and welcoming, no readable text or logos.
- `destinations/vancouver.webp` — Photorealistic editorial travel photograph of Vancouver, modern waterfront city framed by evergreen trees and mountain peaks, soft morning light, balanced city-and-nature composition, no readable text or logos.

All assets are rendered through `next/image` with responsive sizing and descriptive alt text. The overlaid destination labels and itinerary cues are HTML UI, not text baked into the images.
